We have been cupping whatever the roastery drops off on Wednesday mornings for about six months, standing up, in the twenty minutes before opening. Enough people have wandered in mid-slurp and asked what on earth we were doing that we are making it an actual thing.

## First Wednesday of the month, 09:00

Eight seats around the back table. Free. You do not need to know anything and there is no tasting vocabulary test.

Here is what happens: six bowls, ground coffee, hot water, four minutes, break the crust, then everyone slurps off a spoon and says what they think. Some of it will taste like fruit. One will probably taste like a wet paper bag and we will explain why. It takes fifty minutes and there is a flat white at the end of it.

- Turn up on the day — no booking, first eight through the door.
- Do not wear perfume or aftershave, it wrecks it for everyone.
- Coffee on the table is whatever arrived that week, so no two sessions match.

## Autumn, provisionally

Three things are close to confirmed, and are listed here before they are listed anywhere else:

**Wheel session with Hafida Tazi** — a Saturday morning in October, eight seats, ninety minutes. You make a cup, we fire it, you collect it a fortnight later.

**Mending afternoon** — bring anything of ours, or anything at all, that has gone through at the elbow or lost a button. A tailor from up the road is coming with a machine and we are covering her time. Late October, a Sunday, free.

**Long table dinner** — one night, twenty seats, one menu, cooked by whoever we can talk into it. This is the least confirmed of the three because we do not have a kitchen extraction system worth the name. November, optimistically.

Dates land in the newsletter and on this page first. If you want to be told about the wheel session specifically, leave your email at the counter and we will write it on the pad by the till — which remains, a year in, the most reliable booking system we have.
